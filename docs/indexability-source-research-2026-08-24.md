# Indexability source research

Date reviewed: 2026-08-24

## Scope and publication standard

This review covers all 43 paths in `src/lib/seo/noindex-pages.ts` and all 20 expanded FAQ routes in `src/data/faq-expanded.ts`. Thirty-nine noindex paths are editorial pages or public tools that need claim-level sourcing. Four are intentionally non-editorial. A legal, confirmation, or extension utility page does not become index-worthy by adding filler.

The source standard for remediation should be:

- Put a primary source immediately after every material factual claim. A generic exchange homepage is not evidence for a fee, asset listing, jurisdiction, insurance policy, or regulatory status.
- Date all market data, prices, fees, reserves, product availability, circulating supply, rankings, and jurisdictional claims. Prefer a reproducible API response or official register over prose.
- Separate protocol capability from real-world adoption. XRPL documentation can prove that a feature exists, but it cannot prove that an insurer, bank, game, or institution uses it.
- Separate a court's holding from a broader classification. The Ripple judgment did not declare every XRP transaction everywhere to be outside securities law.
- Remove investment recommendations, projected returns, superlatives, invented savings, and adoption claims unless the page publishes a transparent methodology and direct evidence.
- For exchange and wallet comparison pages, retain an evidence snapshot with test date, region, account tier, fee tier, asset/network, deposit and withdrawal status, and the exact official support page reviewed.

## Indexing readiness rule

A route should move from noindex only after its factual corrections are merged, every material claim has a claim-adjacent primary citation, dynamic facts show an “as of” date, comparison methodology is visible, and rendered output exposes citations to users and crawlers. Passing a build or adding a bibliography alone does not establish content quality. The four non-editorial routes above should remain noindex unless their product purpose changes.
