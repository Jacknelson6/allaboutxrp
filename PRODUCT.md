# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

AllAboutXRP serves people trying to understand XRP and the XRP Ledger without promotional noise: newcomers learning the fundamentals, holders checking claims and live data, and experienced readers researching technical, legal, market, and ecosystem topics. They may arrive from search with one urgent question or return to monitor a developing topic, and they need a useful answer before deeper context.

## Product Purpose

AllAboutXRP is an independent, source-led reference for XRP education, live data, practical tools, and curated coverage. It exists to make important claims easier to understand and verify. Success means readers reach a clear answer quickly, can inspect the evidence behind it, understand what is current or uncertain, and continue along a coherent learning or research path.

## Brand Personality

Clear, independent, rigorous. The experience should feel calm enough for careful reading, confident enough to guide a newcomer, and transparent enough that a skeptical reader can verify material claims.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a speculative crypto casino, exchange acquisition funnel, neon token dashboard, anonymous content farm, or pay-to-play affiliate review site. Avoid hype, price guarantees, unsourced adoption claims, decorative purple gradients, glassmorphism, walls of identical cards, and editorial styling that obscures the answer.

## Design Principles

1. Answer first, then earn trust with context and evidence.
2. Make sources, review dates, methodology, and uncertainty visible near the claims they support.
3. Organize the site around real reader intent and canonical learning paths, not page volume.
4. Keep editorial information distinct from live data, tools, and time-sensitive news.
5. Use visual hierarchy and progressive disclosure to welcome newcomers without limiting expert depth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA at minimum. Preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic navigation and headings, descriptive link text, persistent form labels, sufficient color contrast, 44px minimum interactive targets, reduced-motion support, and readable long-form line lengths across mobile and desktop layouts.
